How to Play

Goal: be the last survivor. 24 players drop in, the storm shrinks, and you fight to be #1.

Move: WASD or arrow keys. On touch, drag the left side to move.

Aim & Shoot: aim with the mouse, hold Left Click or Space to fire. On touch, hold the right side to fire at the nearest enemy.

Reload: R. Swap weapon slot: Q / Tab or 1 2.

Trade guns: walk over a gun on the ground to swap it with the one in your hand. Rarer guns (green < blue < purple < gold) do more damage.

Heal: walk over green Health boxes and blue Shield boxes.

Headshots deal double damage. Storm hurts you if you are caught outside the safe circle.

Coins: the higher you place and the more kills you get, the more coins you earn. Your League adds a coin multiplier. Spend coins in the Shop.
